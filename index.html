Voxelings — Privacy Policy

  • Effective date: November 16, 2026
  • Developer: Denis Buchkov
  • App: Voxelings for Android, later for iOS
  • App ID: com.budekong.voxelyata

1. In short

The game itself does not collect personal data and does not require an account. Your progress is stored only on your device.

The game shows Unity Ads. The ads are not personalized. To show them, the ad network receives technical data about the device (see “Third-party services”).

2. What data is collected

The game does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

  • There are no accounts and no login.
  • There is no third-party analytics.
  • The device advertising identifier is not used: the permission for it has been removed from the app.

Stored on your device:

  • your collection of voxelings;
  • the names you give them;
  • cubes and outfits;
  • your settings and the language you chose.

This data is not sent anywhere and is deleted together with the app.

3. Ads

The game contains ads:

  • short videos for a bonus — only when you tap the “Video” button;
  • ads between eggs.

The ads are not personalized: the ad network is told that the app is intended for children, and no interest profile is built.

4. Children

Voxelings is suitable for children, including children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect data from children. The game itself does not collect personal data from anyone, whatever their age.

Ads are shown in a mode for a child audience: without personalization and without the advertising identifier. This is how we follow the children's privacy rules of COPPA (the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and of the GDPR (the EU General Data Protection Regulation).

5. Android permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load ads.
  • Notifications — for a single local reminder that an egg is ready. The reminder is created on the device itself, without any server. You can decline this permission.
  • Vibration — the device vibrates when you interact with the game, to make it feel more immersive.

The game has no access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or photos.

6. Third-party services

Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) — shows the ads. To show ads and to protect against fraud, Unity receives technical data:

  • device model and operating system version;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country, city);
  • information about ad views and taps;
  • diagnostic data.

Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

The app store (RuStore, Google Play or the App Store) processes data about downloads under its own policy.

7. Links to external websites

The settings screen has a link to this policy. It opens in the browser only after a question for grown-ups.

8. How to delete your data

Delete the app — the game's data is removed from your device together with it.

For data processed by Unity Ads, send a request to Unity using the link to their policy in “Third-party services”.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact
