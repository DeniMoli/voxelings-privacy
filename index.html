Voxelings — Privacy Policy

  • Effective date: November 16, 2026
  • Developer: Denis Buchkov
  • App: Voxelings for Android and iOS
  • App ID: com.budekong.voxelyata (Android), com.budekong.voxelings (iOS)

1. In short

The game does not ask for your name, email or phone number and does not require an account. Your progress is stored only on your device.

The game uses third-party services: Unity Ads (non-personalized ads), and Firebase usage statistics and crash reports. They receive technical data about the device and anonymous information about how the game is used (see “Third-party services”).

2. What data is collected

The game does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

  • There are no accounts and no login.
  • The device advertising identifier is not used: on Android the permission for it has been removed from the app, and on iOS the game does not ask for permission to track.
  • The names you give your voxelings, and any other text you type, are not sent anywhere.

Sent to third-party services:

  • a random app installation identifier (it is not linked to your name and changes after you reinstall the app);
  • game events without names or text — for example “egg painted”, “voxeling hatched”, “lair opened”;
  • device model, operating system version, app version and language;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country, city);
  • information about ad views and taps;
  • information about app crashes.

Stored on your device:

  • your collection of voxelings;
  • the names you give them;
  • cubes and outfits;
  • your settings and the language you chose.

This data stays on your device and is deleted together with the app.

3. Ads

The game contains ads:

  • short videos for a bonus — only when you tap the “Video” button;
  • ads between eggs.

The ads are not personalized: the ad network is told that the app is intended for children, and no interest profile is built.

4. Purchases

The Google Play and App Store versions have one purchase, “Remove ads”. It turns off the ads between eggs. The payment is handled by the app store; the game does not receive card details or any other payment data. The purchase opens only after a question for grown-ups.

The RuStore version has no purchases.

5. Children

Voxelings is suitable for children, including children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal details from children: the game does not ask anyone for a name, age, email or phone number, whatever their age.

Ads are shown in a mode for a child audience: without personalization and without the advertising identifier. Usage statistics are collected without the advertising identifier and are not used for advertising. This is how we follow the children's privacy rules of COPPA (the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and of the GDPR (the EU General Data Protection Regulation).

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load ads and to send statistics and crash reports.
  • Notifications — for a single local reminder that an egg is ready. The reminder is created on the device itself, without any server. You can decline this permission.
  • Vibration (Android) — the device vibrates when you interact with the game.

The game has no access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or photos. On iOS the game does not ask for permission to track.

7. Third-party services

Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) — shows the ads and monitors their quality. To show ads and to protect against fraud, Unity receives technical data:

  • device model and operating system version;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country, city);
  • information about ad views and taps;
  • diagnostic data.

Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — usage statistics and crash reports. We use them to see which parts of the game work badly and to fix errors. Google receives:

  • a random app installation identifier;
  • game events without names or text;
  • device model, operating system version, app version and language;
  • IP address and the country derived from it;
  • information about crashes.

The advertising identifier is not collected, and the data is not used for advertising. Statistics are kept for no longer than 14 months, crash reports for 90 days.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

The app store (RuStore, Google Play or the App Store) processes data about downloads and purchases under its own policy.

8. Links to external websites

The settings screen has a link to this policy. It opens in the browser only after a question for grown-ups.

9. How to delete your data

Delete the app — the game's data is removed from your device together with it. The installation identifier is no longer used after that; a new installation gets a different one.

To delete statistics already sent to Firebase, write to us at the address in “Contact”.

For data processed by Unity Ads, send a request to Unity using the link to their policy in “Third-party services”.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
