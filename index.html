Voxelings — Privacy Policy

  • Effective date: November 16, 2026
  • Developer: Denis Buchkov
  • App: Voxelings for Android, later for iOS
  • App ID: com.budekong.voxelyata

1. In short

Voxelings does not collect or share any personal data. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

2. What data we collect

None. The game does not collect personal data and does not send it anywhere.

  • There are no accounts, no sign-up and no login.
  • There are no ads, no third-party analytics and no advertising identifiers.
  • There are no in-app purchases.
  • The game has no links to external websites or social networks.

Your game progress is stored only on your device:

  • your collection;
  • the names you give to creatures;
  • your settings;
  • the language you chose.

This progress is not copied to any server, and there is no server backup. It is deleted together with the app.

3. Children

Voxelings is suitable for children, including children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect data from children. In fact, the game does not collect personal data from anyone, whatever their age.

Because no personal data is collected, the game is consistent with the children's privacy rules of COPPA (the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and of the GDPR (the EU General Data Protection Regulation).

4. Device permissions

On Android the game uses two permissions:

  • Notifications — for a single local reminder that an egg is ready. The reminder is created on the device itself, without any server. You can decline this permission.
  • Vibration — the device vibrates when you interact with the game, to make it feel more immersive.

The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or photos.

5. Third-party services

The game does not use any third-party services.

One thing to know: the app store you download the game from — Google Play or the App Store — processes data about downloads under its own privacy policy. We do not control this. You can read their policies here: Google, Apple.

6. How to delete your data

Delete the app. All game progress is removed from your device together with it. There is nothing else to delete, because nothing is stored on a server.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to us: denibuchkov@gmail.com
